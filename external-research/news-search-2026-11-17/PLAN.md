## Scope

**A full audit** (all dimensions D1 to D8 plus D10, every paper and tool, the citation-audit hook) with two changes from earlier full rounds:

- **The auditing line and its related lines come first.** Their lanes launch first, their candidates verify first, and the Workflow sweep is mostly theirs. If quota or the time box runs out, the general long tail is what waits.
- **High-value evidence only** (new rule in `skills/news-search/SKILL.md`, "High-Value Evidence Only"). Lanes report Tier 0 to Tier 2, Ledger 6 and Ledger 7 material, and the high-value Ledger 3 types (patents, theses, books, university courses, enterprise documents, organization-owned code and integrations, specifications, datasets, and documents that benchmark or compare against a lab work). Blog posts, tutorials, aggregator pages, personal repositories, curated lists, forum posts and platform listings are not searched for or verified.

Search window: items dated from 2026-10-15 for news-type surfaces (the 10-21 full round and the 11-09 incremental round covered earlier items), all dates for academic citers, code, patents and theses (suppressed against the record), and documents published or revised from 2026-07-01 for PDF deep search.

Citation-audit hook: `citation-affiliation-audit.md` was generated 2026-10-21, inside the 30-day window, so Phase B writes the summary and does not rerun it.

Launch, from the repository root, in the background, with the 45 IDs written out literally in `units.txt` order (auditing first):


Wait mode keeps `--cap` in force; the command's exit wakes the coordinator. A lane is complete only with a present, non-empty result, no FALLBACK header, and a recorded exit of 0. Each prompt asks the worker to rewrite its result file every 20 queries, so a timed-out lane keeps its partial work.

It appends each verdict to its own file as it goes. Whatever a wave leaves unfinished is logged by identifier for the next round.
