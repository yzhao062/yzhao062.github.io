Dr. Yue Zhao is an Assistant Professor of Computer Science at the University of Southern California, where he leads the FORTIS Lab. He serves as Associate Co-Director of the USC Institute on Ethics and Trust in Computing for 2026-2027. He received his Ph.D. from Carnegie Mellon University's Heinz College of Information Systems and Public Policy in 2023. His lab builds Auditable AI Systems across data, outputs, actions, and effort, making AI behavior easier to inspect, verify, account for, and optimize. The program spans anomaly detection, LLM evaluation, and the security and efficiency of AI agents, with a current focus on open-source infrastructure for auditing autonomous agents before deployment and after a run.
Dr. Zhao has authored over 80 peer-reviewed papers. His open-source projects, including PyOD, ADBench, TrustLLM, agent-audit, and Aegis, together exceed 65 million downloads and 30,000 GitHub stars. PyOD is named by OpenAI, used by Apache Beam, Amazon, Walmart, Databricks, and the European Space Agency, and cited in 94 patents and five books. TrustLLM is cited in a U.S. Senate committee report, a NIST special publication on adversarial machine learning, and the 2025 and 2026 editions of the International AI Safety Report.
His honors include the NVIDIA Academic Grant Program Award, multiple Amazon Research Awards, the Capital One Research Award, and the Foresight Institute AI for Safety & Science Nodes Grant. A Meta sponsored research agreement also supports the lab. AAAI selected him for New Faculty Highlights, and his papers have won a Best Short Paper Award at ACM SIGSPATIAL and a Second Prize CCC Award at the IEEE ICDM BlueSky Track. He is an Action Editor for Transactions on Machine Learning Research and the Journal of Data-centric Machine Learning Research, and an Associate Editor for ACM Transactions on AI for Science and IEEE Transactions on Neural Networks and Learning Systems.
